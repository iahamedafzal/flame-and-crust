# 🔥 Flame & Crust

### Pizza. Burgers. Made to Order.

Flame & Crust is a modern food ordering mobile app UI/UX prototype designed to provide a simple, engaging, and visually appealing food-ordering experience.

The app focuses on popular food categories, today's special items, menu exploration, and an easy ordering journey.

## 📱 About the App

**Flame & Crust** is designed as a mobile food ordering application focused on popular fast-food items such as:

- 🍕 Pizza
- 🍔 Burgers
- 🥤 Drinks
- 🌯 Shawarma

The interface uses a warm food-inspired visual style with dark brown, burnt-orange, cream, and gold tones to create a strong brand identity.

---

## ✨ Main Features

### 🔐 Login

Users can:

- Enter their email address
- Enter their password
- Log in to their account
- Continue as a guest
- Access the "Forgot password?" option
- Create a new account

---

### 🏠 Home

The home screen provides:

- Personalized greeting
- Search bar
- Popular food categories
- Today's special
- Featured food item
- Quick access to food discovery

Example:

**Today's Special**
> Pepperoni Melt  
> Extra cheese. Spicy  
> ₹249

---

### 🔎 Food Search

The search interface allows users to search for food items such as:

- Pizza
- Burgers
- Drinks
- Shawarma

The search bar is designed to provide quick access to the desired food.

---

### 🍕 Popular Categories

The home screen presents popular categories including:

| Category | Example |
|---|---|
| 🍕 Pizza | Pizza varieties |
| 🍔 Burgers | Burger options |

| 🌯 Shawarma | Shawarma options |

### 📋 Menu

The menu screen allows users to browse different food items.

The interface includes category filters and food cards so users can quickly explore available dishes.
