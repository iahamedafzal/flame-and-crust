# 🔥 Flame & Crust

> **A modern food ordering app UI/UX prototype designed to deliver a simple, attractive, and user-friendly food ordering experience.**

## 📱 Project Overview

**Flame & Crust** is a modern food ordering app prototype designed in **Figma**.

The project focuses on creating a clean and intuitive interface that allows users to explore food items, view details, manage their cart, and access their personal profile.

## ✨ Key Features

* 🏠 **Home** — Explore the food ordering experience
* 🍕 **Food Menu** — Browse available food items
* 🔎 **Food Discovery** — Easily explore different options
* 🛒 **Cart** — Manage selected food items
* 👤 **Profile** — View and manage personal details
* 📱 **Mobile UI** — Designed for a smooth mobile experience
* 🎨 **Modern Interface** — Clean and visually appealing design
